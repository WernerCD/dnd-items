D&D Item Finder

Magic items and gear for 2014 and 2024 D&D 5e rules

Changes

What has changed on the Item Finder, newest first. Back to the Item Finder.

November 13, 2026

  • Tidier 2024 list. The 2024 rules list each weapon or armor version of some items separately (Flame Tongue Longsword, Demon Plate Armor and so on). Those now fold into one card per item, like the 2014 ones: Flame Tongue, Vicious Weapon, Demon Armor, Dancing Sword, Vorpal Sword, Berserker Axe and Elven Chain. The footer also links the new Spell Planner.
  • Print and polish. Printing the page now gives a clean list of the item cards, without the filters (tick Favorites only first for a printed shopping or loot list). Links in the header and footer are easier to tap, a missing page shows a proper "Page not found" page, and shared links show a title and description.
  • Update notice. If the site is updated while you have it open, a small bar offers to reload so you get the new version.
  • Consistent design. The change log moved to its own page, and the header, footer and theme are now shared across the D&D Toolbox sites.
  • D&D Toolbox. A link back to the D&D Toolbox home page, at the top of the page and in About.
  • Faster, tidier. Opening an item is now instant even with every card loaded (it took about 2 seconds with the whole list showing). Tables inside quoted rules text and bold-italic labels such as Injury. on poisons now display properly. Poison names are capitalised, and Acid, Oil, Torch and Net in the 2024 rules count as adventuring gear. About 80 book entries that were the same item under another name (Hooded Lantern = Lantern, Hooded; Silk Rope = Rope, silk) are merged into the SRD item instead of listed twice.
  • Summaries, shareable filters. Every SRD item has a short original summary on its Stats and Full cards and in its detail view, with separate 2014 wording where the rules differ. The link now keeps your filters (kind, type, rarity, attunement, consumable, books, view and sort), so a filtered list can be bookmarked or shared. The Type and Rarity menus have Clear buttons, and more cards load as you scroll.
  • Fixes. Picking a rarity or type that left a single variant of a grouped item broke the page, so the filters and the Basic / Stats / Full switch stopped responding; fixed. Type and rarity counts now match what you get with your other filters. Consumables is now a menu with a Non-consumables option. Select all / Clear all cover the books of both editions. When searching, items with the word in their name come first.
  • Rarity colours and book items. Cards, rarity tags and the Rarity menu share one colour per rarity. Basic gear, armor and weapons now count for the book that prints them too (for example Chain Mail shows Player's Handbook p.219 as well as SRD 5.2), and generic variants such as Mariner's Armor and Armor of Gleaming are in the index.
  • Core books first. The Sources picker lists the core rulebooks first (SRDs, Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide, Monster Manual, Xanathar's, Tasha's) and starts with only those selected; a Core books button goes back to that. Filter menus no longer slip behind the cards on phones.
  • Launch. Every SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2 item with full text, for 2014 and 2024 rules: magic items, weapons, armor, adventuring gear, tools, poisons, potions and scrolls. Search, filters for type, rarity, attunement and consumables, a Sources picker, Basic / Stats / Full cards, grouped variants, favorites, an index of items from other official books, source material links and this change log.
