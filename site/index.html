About

Search every item in the free D&D 5e System Reference Documents, with full rules text, for the 2014 rules (SRD 5.1) and the 2024 rules (SRD 5.2). Items from the other official books are listed as an index: name, type, rarity, attunement, book and page, so you know where to look them up.

Item text marked SRD 5.1 or SRD 5.2 comes from the System Reference Documents 5.1 and 5.2 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at dnd.wizards.com and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. It was retrieved through the Open5e API. The index of items from other books (facts only, no rules text) was compiled from the community 5etools dataset. Unofficial fan content; not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.

    Change log

    1. 2026-11-13 Faster, tidier. Opening an item is now instant even with every card loaded (it took about 2 seconds with the whole list showing). Tables inside quoted rules text and bold-italic labels such as Injury. on poisons now display properly. Poison names are capitalised, and Acid, Oil, Torch and Net in the 2024 rules count as adventuring gear. About 80 book entries that were the same item under another name (Hooded Lantern = Lantern, Hooded; Silk Rope = Rope, silk) are merged into the SRD item instead of listed twice.
    2. 2026-11-13 Summaries, shareable filters. Every SRD item has a short original summary on its Stats and Full cards and in its detail view, with separate 2014 wording where the rules differ. The link now keeps your filters (kind, type, rarity, attunement, consumable, books, view and sort), so a filtered list can be bookmarked or shared. The Type and Rarity menus have Clear buttons, and more cards load as you scroll.
    3. 2026-11-13 Fixes. Picking a rarity or type that left a single variant of a grouped item broke the page, so the filters and the Basic / Stats / Full switch stopped responding; fixed. Type and rarity counts now match what you get with your other filters. Consumables is now a menu with a Non-consumables option. Select all / Clear all cover the books of both editions. When searching, items with the word in their name come first.
    4. 2026-11-13 Rarity colours and book items. Cards, rarity tags and the Rarity menu share one colour per rarity. Basic gear, armor and weapons now count for the book that prints them too (for example Chain Mail shows Player's Handbook p.219 as well as SRD 5.2), and generic variants such as Mariner's Armor and Armor of Gleaming are in the index.
    5. 2026-11-13 Core books first. The Sources picker lists the core rulebooks first (SRDs, Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide, Monster Manual, Xanathar's, Tasha's) and starts with only those selected; a Core books button goes back to that. Filter menus no longer slip behind the cards on phones.
    6. 2026-11-13 Launch. Every SRD 5.1 and SRD 5.2 item with full text, for 2014 and 2024 rules: magic items, weapons, armor, adventuring gear, tools, poisons, potions and scrolls. Search, filters for type, rarity, attunement and consumables, a Sources picker, Basic / Stats / Full cards, grouped variants, favorites, an index of items from other official books, source material links and this change log.
