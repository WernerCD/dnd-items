About

Search every item in the free D&D 5e System Reference Documents, with full rules text, for the 2014 rules (SRD 5.1) and the 2024 rules (SRD 5.2). Items from the other official books are listed as an index: name, type, rarity, attunement, book and page, so you know where to look them up.

Item text marked SRD 5.1 or SRD 5.2 comes from the System Reference Documents 5.1 and 5.2 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at dnd.wizards.com and licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. It was retrieved through the Open5e API. The index of items from other books (facts only, no rules text) was compiled from the community 5etools dataset.
